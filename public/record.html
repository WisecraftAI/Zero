Record session

Record your actions on the OTT app so requirements and locators can be improved.

1. Open your OTT app in a new tab: Open OTT URL
2. Drag this link to your bookmarks bar, then on the OTT page click the bookmark: Start ZER0 recorder
3. Use the app. A green Stop ZER0 recording button will appear at the bottom-right of the OTT page.
4. When done, click Stop ZER0 recording on the OTT page, then return here.
